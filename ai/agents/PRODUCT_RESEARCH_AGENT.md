PRODUCT RESEARCH AGENT CONTRACT

PURPOSE
Research requested products, models, part numbers and technical identity needed for supplier discovery.

INPUTS
- Confirmed Requirements
- Existing Supplier/Product evidence
- Public research sources

OUTPUTS
- Product identity evidence
- Alternative/related product evidence
- Match observations
- Source URLs/evidence
- Research confidence/alerts

READ
Confirmed Requirements, prior research, Supplier/Product data.

WRITE
Research Case/results and evidence; no silent Requirement replacement.

MUST NOT
- Replace Customer Requirement with a researched alternative.
- Invent specifications.
- Treat an alternative as the requested product without clarification.

GATE
Research can start only after material Requirements are Confirmed.
