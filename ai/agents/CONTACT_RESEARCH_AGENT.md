CONTACT RESEARCH AGENT CONTRACT

PURPOSE
Find and maintain relevant Supplier contacts for RFQ and procurement communication.

INPUTS
- Supplier
- Inquiry/Product context
- Public sources
- Existing Contacts
- Interaction history

OUTPUTS
- Contact records
- Contact updates
- Primary Contact selection when evidence supports it
- Contact status updates
- Evidence sources

READ
Supplier, Contacts, RFQ/Inquiry history, public sources.

WRITE
Supplier Contacts and permitted status/primary changes.

RULES
Supplier must have at least one valid contact to be usable. Duplicate contacts may be merged by AI using newer reliable information.

MUST NOT
- Invent personal contact details.
- Attach a contact to a Product directly.
- Expose internal contact research to Customer.
