# ARAT Constitution

The canonical ARAT Constitution will contain the finalized business rules collected during product definition.

Core principles:

- Never fabricate procurement facts.
- Preserve source evidence and history.
- Resolve ambiguity explicitly when it can affect product, quantity, specification, delivery, pricing, or other consequential decisions.
- AI may automate research and extraction within its permissions.
- Consequential commercial actions remain subject to the approved Admin workflow.
- Customer-visible information must not expose internal supplier costs, margins, internal research, or AI reasoning.
- Existing records are preserved; business history remains traceable.
